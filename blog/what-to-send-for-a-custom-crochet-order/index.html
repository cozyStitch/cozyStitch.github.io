What to Send for a Custom Crochet Order

The useful details that help us understand your custom cozyStitch request quickly and accurately.

Aliza Arshad•July 12, 2026•0 min read

A custom order is easier to plan when the important details arrive together. Fortunately, nobody needs a thirty-page presentation about a keychain.

Tell us what you want made

Start with the product type: a hair clip, keychain, bag charm, mini handbag, crochet flower, character charm, gift, or something different.

Share colors and references

If you have preferred colors, mention them clearly. Reference images are also helpful when you are describing a character, flower, style, outfit, or theme. You can send images directly in WhatsApp after submitting the custom-order form.

Mention size and quantity

Size matters for some pieces, especially mini bags and character designs. Quantity also affects preparation time, so include it from the beginning when possible.

Tell us what matters most

If one detail is especially important, say so. It might be a specific color, facial expression, flower shape, attachment style, or gift date.

Once we understand the request, we can confirm what is possible, pricing, and an estimated preparation time before work begins.
